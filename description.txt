Chopped trees now topple over with real physics instead of vanishing into a pile of logs.
Falling trunks can hit and damage players/NPCs, then remain as a physical obstacle on the ground
that can be chopped up for wood.

деревья джунглей и т.п. можно добавить по аналогии — см. комментарии в коде.
